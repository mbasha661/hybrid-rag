How it works

19 passages from the PDF were embedded offline with the free bge-small-en-v1.5 model. Your question is embedded in the browser with the same model (transformers.js, one-time ~30 MB download), BM25 scores exact words, and reciprocal rank fusion merges both rankings. With a free Groq key (console.groq.com) the top passages go to Groq's free tier for a written answer with page citations; without a key you get the passages themselves. The key never leaves your browser except to api.groq.com.
